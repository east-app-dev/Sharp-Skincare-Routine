How AI skin analysis works

What a face scan on your phone can actually measure, how to get results you can compare week to week, and where it stops being useful.

By Esat Karabiyik, SharpUpdated 2026-11-215 min read
TL;DRAn AI skin scan analyzes a photo of your face for visible features like oil, redness, pores, blemishes and under-eye shadows and turns them into scores. It’s great for tracking cosmetic changes over time under consistent conditions. It is not a medical diagnosis.

What the scan looks at

AI skin analysis uses computer vision on a photo of your face. The model looks for visible patterns and assigns them to facial zones. In Sharp, that results in six scores from 0 to 100:

ScoreWhat it reflects
ClarityVisible blemishes, spots and breakouts
Oil & sebumShine, mainly across the T-zone
RednessVisible redness and irritation, e.g. after shaving
Pores & textureVisible pores and unevenness
Eye areaDark circles and puffiness
HydrationSigns of dryness and flaking

The overall Skin Score combines these. Higher means fewer visible concerns.

Why consistency matters more than accuracy

Light, distance, angle and even camera exposure change how skin looks in a photo. That’s why one scan on its own says less than a series. Sharp guides the capture, checks lighting and head position and fires the shutter itself, so each weekly scan is taken under similar conditions. The trend is the signal.

Tips for reliable scans

  • Scan at the same time of day, ideally in the morning before products.
  • Face a window or soft light; avoid strong light from one side.
  • Clean face, no beard trim or shave right before the scan.
  • Same room, same distance, every week.

What AI skin analysis can’t do

  • It can’t diagnose conditions such as rosacea, eczema or skin cancer.
  • It can’t see below the surface or judge what a mole is.
  • It can be thrown off by bad lighting, filters or makeup.

Use it as a cosmetic tracker and a routine coach. For anything that hurts, bleeds, spreads or changes, see a dermatologist.

From scan to routine

The useful part isn’t the number, it’s what you do with it. Sharp turns the scan into an 8-week morning and evening routine, re-scans weekly and adapts the plan when a score doesn’t move.

FAQ

Are AI skin analysis apps accurate?

They are good at spotting and tracking visible features under consistent conditions. Absolute numbers vary with lighting, so compare scans taken the same way.

Can an app diagnose acne or rosacea?

No. Skin analysis apps give cosmetic estimates. Diagnosis and treatment of skin conditions belong with a dermatologist.

How often should I scan my face?

Once a week is enough. Skin changes slowly, and weekly scans under the same conditions show real trends.
