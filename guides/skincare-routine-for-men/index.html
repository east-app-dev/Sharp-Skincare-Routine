The simple skincare routine for men

Three steps, under three minutes a day. What to use, in which order, and how to adjust it to your skin type.

By Esat Karabiyik, SharpUpdated 2026-11-215 min read
TL;DRMorning: cleanse, moisturize, sunscreen (SPF 30+). Evening: cleanse, moisturize. Pick products by skin type, not by “for men” labels. Give any change 4 to 8 weeks before judging it.

The 3-step routine

Dermatologists keep the basics simple: clean skin, keep it hydrated, protect it from the sun. A routine you actually do every day beats a perfect one you drop after a week.

StepMorningEvening
1. CleanseGentle face wash, lukewarm waterGentle face wash, removes sweat, oil and sunscreen
2. MoisturizeLight, non-comedogenic moisturizerSame moisturizer, or a slightly richer one if skin is dry
3. ProtectBroad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or higherNot needed

1. Cleanse twice a day

Wash in the morning, in the evening and after training. Use a gentle cleanser and lukewarm water; hot water and harsh scrubs strip the skin and can make oiliness and redness worse. Pat dry, don’t rub.

2. Moisturize, even if your skin is oily

Moisturizer keeps the skin barrier intact. Oily skin still needs it, just lighter: look for gel or lotion textures labeled “oil-free” or “non-comedogenic”. Dry skin does better with a cream.

3. Sunscreen every morning

UV exposure is the biggest driver of visible skin aging and raises skin cancer risk. A broad-spectrum SPF 30+ moisturizer or sunscreen as the last morning step covers most days.

Adjust it to your skin type

  • Oily: foaming or gel cleanser, oil-free moisturizer. See the oily skin guide.
  • Dry: cream cleanser, richer moisturizer, avoid alcohol-heavy aftershave.
  • Combination: oily T-zone, dry cheeks. Light moisturizer everywhere, a bit more on dry areas.
  • Sensitive or prone to razor bumps: fragrance-free products, shave with the grain, use a sharp blade.

Shaving fits into step 1

Shave after cleansing, when hair is soft. Use a shaving cream or gel, shave in the direction of hair growth, rinse with cool water and moisturize afterwards. Replace blades regularly.

When to add more

Only once the basics are a habit. Common additions are an exfoliating acid (salicylic acid for oily, breakout-prone skin) or a retinoid at night. Add one product at a time so you can tell what works.

How to know it’s working

Skin changes slowly; most routines need 4 to 8 weeks. Take photos under the same light every week, or use a scan app like Sharp that scores your skin weekly under consistent conditions, so you compare like with like instead of guessing from the mirror.

FAQ

How many steps should a men’s skincare routine have?

Three is enough for most men: cleanser, moisturizer and sunscreen in the morning, cleanser and moisturizer at night.

Do men need different products than women?

Not really. Choose by skin type (oily, dry, combination, sensitive) and concerns, not by gendered marketing.

How long until I see results?

Usually 4 to 8 weeks of consistent use. Change one thing at a time so you know what made the difference.
