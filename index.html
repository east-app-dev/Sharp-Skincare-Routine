FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Sharp?

Sharp is an iPhone app for men that analyzes facial skin with AI. A 10-second face scan gives you a skin score from 0 to 100 across six measures (clarity, oil, redness, pores and texture, eye area and hydration) and turns it into a personal 8-week morning and evening routine.

How does the AI skin scan work?

You hold your iPhone at eye level and follow the on-screen guide. Sharp checks lighting and head position, captures your face and evaluates visible skin features zone by zone. The result is six scores and an overall Skin Score. Weekly re-scans under the same conditions show how your skin changes.

Is Sharp free?

Downloading Sharp and your first scan with your skin score are free. The full routine, weekly re-scans, progress tracking and product check are part of a subscription, with a one-time lifetime option available in the app.

Is Sharp a medical app or a dermatologist replacement?

No. Sharp gives cosmetic skincare guidance and does not diagnose or treat skin conditions. If you have painful, persistent or severe acne, a changing mole or any skin concern that worries you, see a board-certified dermatologist.

Which skin concerns does Sharp cover?

Everyday cosmetic concerns most men care about: oily or shiny skin, breakouts and blackheads, redness and razor irritation, visible pores and texture, tired eyes and dark circles, and dry or dehydrated skin.

Can Sharp tell me if a skincare product fits my skin?

Yes. With product check you scan a product with the camera. Sharp reads the label and gives a match score based on your own scan and goals, explains why, and suggests a better fit if needed.

What happens to my face photos?

Your scans are processed to compute your skin analysis and are handled under the EU GDPR. Details on processing, storage and deletion are in the privacy policy.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Sharp runs on iPhone with iOS 15.5 or later and is available in English and German.
