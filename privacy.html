2. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we process when you use the Sharp app, for what purposes, on what legal basis, and what rights you have.

In short:

  • Sharp processes account data, quiz responses, face photos, product photos, and analysis results.
  • Face and product photos are processed only transiently: they are sent to an AI service for analysis and deleted from our servers immediately afterwards.
  • We collect pseudonymous usage statistics in our own database to improve the App. We do not use third-party analytics services such as Google Analytics or Mixpanel.
  • Only if you allow it in Apple's tracking dialog ("Allow Tracking"), we use the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure which of our ads led to your install and purchase. If you do not allow it, no data is sent to Meta.
  • We do not sell your data and do not show ads inside the App.
  • Sharp does not provide medical diagnoses.

3.4a Face Data: Retention and Sharing at a Glance

Because face photos are sensitive, here is a plain-language summary of exactly how we handle them:

  • Is face data retained? No. We do not retain face data. Face photos are processed transiently and are not stored at rest on our servers. We do not create face templates, faceprints, or any biometric identifiers, and we do not perform face recognition.
  • Why is face data processed and stored at all? A face photo is uploaded for one single reason: to run the automated cosmetic skin analysis that computes your scores. There is no other storage purpose.
  • How long is face data stored, and why this length? The uploaded photo remains on our servers only for the duration of the analysis itself, typically a few seconds, from upload until the analysis has been performed, and is then deleted automatically. We chose this minimal duration because the photo is needed solely as input for the score computation; nothing in the service requires keeping it afterwards. If an analysis cannot be completed (e.g. a network error), the residual upload is deleted automatically with your next scan and, in any event, when you delete your data or account.
  • Which third parties is face data shared with? Two categories only: (1) Supabase Inc. (our hosting provider, as a processor) transiently handles the upload described above; (2) one AI vision provider per analysis, Google LLC (Gemini API) or OpenAI, L.L.C. (OpenAI API), depending on our server configuration. Face data is not sold and is not shared with anyone else, in particular not with Meta or any advertising partner.
  • Why is face data shared with these third parties? Solely so the automated cosmetic analysis can be performed: Supabase provides the infrastructure that receives the upload, and the AI vision provider's model computes the cosmetic scores from the photo. No advertising, profiling, identification, or other purpose.
  • Do these third parties store face data?
    • Supabase: only the transient upload described above, which we delete immediately after the analysis. Supabase does not use the data for any own purposes (processor under Art. 28 GDPR).
    • Google (Gemini API, paid tier): Google does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models. Google retains API prompts (including images) and outputs for up to 55 days solely to detect and prevent violations of its Prohibited Use Policy (abuse monitoring), after which they are deleted. See Google's Gemini API usage policies: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/usage-policies.
    • OpenAI (API): OpenAI does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models. OpenAI retains API abuse-monitoring logs (which may include prompts such as images) for up to 30 days solely to enforce its usage policies and prevent harmful use, after which they are deleted. See OpenAI's API data controls and enterprise privacy commitments: https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/.

3.11 Usage Statistics (Our Own Analytics)

To understand how the App is used and to improve it (e.g. where users drop off in the onboarding, which screens confuse, whether a new version works better), the App records usage events in our own database. We do not use third-party analytics services. An event contains:

  • the event name and context (e.g. "screen viewed", "quiz question answered" with the selected answer option, "paywall viewed", "purchase started/completed" with product and price, "routine block completed"),
  • a random installation ID and session ID, and, once you are signed in, your account ID,
  • app version and build, operating system version, device model (e.g. "iPhone15,2", never the device name you chose), and language/region setting,
  • timestamp.

The events contain no photos, no location, and no free text you typed. Quiz answers are recorded as the predefined answer options you selected.

Storage: Supabase database (events). Only we can read this data; the App can only write it.
Retention: for as long as needed to evaluate App versions, at most 24 months. When you delete your account, the events are detached from your account and remain only under the random installation ID.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the App and its onboarding). You can object at any time by email (Section 12); we will then delete the events linked to your account and installation ID.

3.12 Install Attribution and Advertising Measurement

To understand which of our marketing channels bring users and to measure our own advertising, we use the following. None of it is used to show you ads inside Sharp.

a) Apple Search Ads attribution. On first launch, the App requests an attribution token from Apple (AdServices framework, no advertising identifier needed) and our server exchanges it with Apple for the campaign that led to the install, if any. We store the campaign, ad group and keyword IDs and the country in your profile.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in measuring our marketing).

b) Creator links. If you open Sharp via a creator's referral link, our Creator Hub logs the click with a one-way hash of your IP address (not the IP address itself). On first launch, the App asks the Creator Hub whether a click from the same network happened in the previous 72 hours; if so, the creator's code is stored in your profile so the creator can be credited for a resulting subscription. Creators see only aggregated numbers, never your identity. You can also enter a creator code manually.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in crediting partners).

c) Meta (Facebook) advertising measurement, only with your consent. On first launch, iOS asks whether Sharp may track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). Only if you choose "Allow":

  • the App starts the Meta SDK, which sends app events to Meta (such as app install and app opens), together with your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), a Meta anonymous ID, device and app information, and your IP address;
  • our subscription provider RevenueCat forwards your purchase events (product, price, currency, time) with these identifiers to Meta via Meta's Conversions API;
  • Meta uses this data to tell us which of our ads led to installs and purchases, and to optimize the delivery of our ads.

If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the Meta SDK is not started and no data is sent to Meta. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; withdrawing consent stops all further transfers.

Recipient: Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland. For the collection and transmission of this data, we and Meta are joint controllers (Art. 26 GDPR); Meta's subsequent processing is Meta's own responsibility. Meta may transfer data to Meta Platforms, Inc. in the USA; Meta is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also uses Standard Contractual Clauses. Further information: Meta Privacy Policy, Meta Controller Addendum.

7. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described:

  • face photos and product photos: transient processing only; we do not store them at rest on our servers. An uploaded photo is kept on our servers only for the moment it takes to run the automated analysis and is deleted automatically as soon as the analysis has been performed, typically within seconds of upload. We use this minimal retention period because the photo is needed solely to compute the result; nothing in the service requires the server to keep it afterwards. In the rare case that an analysis cannot be completed (e.g. a network error), the residual upload is deleted automatically with your next scan and, in any event, immediately when you delete your data or account. The photos shown in the App (e.g. before/after comparison, product history) are stored exclusively locally on your device and never leave it except for the transient analysis described above,
  • scan and product check results (numeric scores and short text findings; these contain no facial imagery and are not face data): for as long as your account exists, so the App can track your progress over time,
  • account data, quiz, plans, attribution data: for as long as your account exists,
  • usage statistics: at most 24 months (Section 3.11),
  • subscription/webhook logs: as required by law and for technical necessity,
  • data received by Meta (only with consent): according to Meta's own retention rules (see Meta Privacy Policy),
  • local device data (including your photos for the before/after view): until you delete data in the App or uninstall the App.

8. Deleting Your Data

8.1 "Delete Data" in the App

Under Settings → Delete Data, you can permanently delete your data. After confirmation:

If you are signed in, we delete:

  • your account (Supabase Auth),
  • your profile, onboarding quiz, scan and product check results, plans, product selections, attribution data, and routine history from our database (via cascade delete),
  • all face and product photos remaining in our private cloud storage (face-scans and product-scans buckets),
  • your local data on the device (local photos, app state, and preferences).

Usage statistics are detached from your account and remain only under the random installation ID (Section 3.11). If you want them deleted as well, email us.

If you are not signed in, we delete local data on your device only (local photos and app state).

This action cannot be undone.

If deletion fails (e.g. no network connection), you will see an error message and your data will not be removed until deletion succeeds.

Data already transmitted to Meta with your consent is not deleted by this action; you can manage it via your Meta account or by contacting Meta.

8.2 Sign Out

Sign Out in Settings ends your session only. Your stored server data remains until you use Delete Data or request deletion by email.
